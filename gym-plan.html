NOTES
💪 Muscle Split
→Day A — Chest, Shoulders, Triceps (push day)
→Day B — Back, Biceps, Core (pull day)
→Day C — Legs, Glutes, Core (lower body)
📋 Structure
→Rotate A → B → C, non-consecutive days (e.g. Mon / Wed / Fri)
→Add 10–20 min incline walk or bike after 2 sessions per week
→Compounds: 90s rest. Isolation: 60s rest.
🍗 Nutrition
→~200–300 kcal below maintenance to chip away at belly fat
→~2g protein per kg bodyweight daily to keep your muscle
📈 Progression
→Add weight once you hit the top rep range across all sets
→Machines make progressive overload easy and safe — use them
